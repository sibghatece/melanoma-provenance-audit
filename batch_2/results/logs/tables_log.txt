==========================================================================
CAVEAT ON TABLE 10 -- READ BEFORE SUBMITTING
==========================================================================
Rows were compiled from sources that were retrieved and read, but:

  * Several are PREPRINTS (flagged in source_note). Check whether a
    peer-reviewed version exists and cite that instead.
  * Some rows (Ali et al., Duraes & Vestias, Qasim Gilani, Yilmaz,
    Bechelli & Delhommelle) were read from SURVEY papers quoting those
    results, not from the primary papers. Numbers quoted second-hand are
    occasionally wrong. Open each primary paper, confirm the figure, and
    record full bibliographic details before the table goes into the
    manuscript.
  * The two audit rows (DermaMNIST audit; IEEE Access 2026 leakage audit)
    are prior art for lesion-level leakage. This study REPLICATES that
    finding rather than discovering it, and the manuscript must say so.
  * Do not mix accuracy figures across differing prevalence and split
    protocols without the protocol column visible. That column is the
    point of the table.
==========================================================================
